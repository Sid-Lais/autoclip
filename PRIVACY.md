# AutoClip Privacy Policy

AutoClip is a personal tool run on the owner's own computer. It cuts short video clips from videos the owner chooses and, when the owner approves, uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

**What it accesses.** With the owner's consent it uses the YouTube upload permission (`youtube.upload`) and read-only channel information (`youtube.readonly`) to upload videos and to show the connected channel's name.

**What it stores.** The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

**What it shares.** Nothing. AutoClip does not collect, sell, or share personal data. Video content is processed on the owner's computer; transcript text may be sent to the AI provider the owner configures to choose clip moments.

**Removing access.** Visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove AutoClip, or disconnect YouTube in the app's Settings.
